NY Daily Numbers — officially called Pick 3 — is one of the most popular lottery games in New York State. It's been running twice daily since 1980, making it one of the longest-standing Pick 3 games in the country.

If you've never played before, this guide covers everything you need to know to walk up to a lottery terminal and make an informed play.

---

## What NY Daily Numbers Is

Daily Numbers is a three-digit lottery game. You choose three digits, each from 0 to 9, and try to match the three digits drawn by the NY Lottery.

Draws happen twice every day:
- **Midday draw:** approximately 2:30 PM Eastern Time
- **Evening draw:** approximately 10:30 PM Eastern Time

Both draws happen seven days a week, including holidays. You can play one draw, the other, or both on the same ticket.

## The Five Bet Types

This is where most beginners get confused. Pick 3 has five different ways to play, each with different rules, different odds, and different payouts.

### Straight

You win only if your three digits match the drawn numbers in the **exact order** you chose.

If you play 4-7-2 straight, you win only on a draw of 4-7-2. A draw of 7-4-2 or 2-7-4 does not win on a straight ticket.

**Payout:** $500 on a $1 play | **Odds:** 1 in 1,000

### Box

You win if your three digits match in **any order**.

If you play 4-7-2 box, you win on any arrangement: 4-7-2, 4-2-7, 7-4-2, 7-2-4, 2-4-7, or 2-7-4.

There are two box types:
- **6-way box** — all three digits are distinct (like 4-7-2). Six possible arrangements. Pays $80 on $1.
- **3-way box** — two of your three digits are the same (like 4-4-7). Only three arrangements exist. Pays $160 on $1.

**Odds:** 1 in 167 (6-way) or 1 in 333 (3-way)

### Straight/Box

A combination bet on one ticket. You win the straight prize if your digits match in exact order. You win the box prize if they match in any other order.

A $1 Straight/Box play: exact match pays $290 + $40 = $330. Any-order match pays $40 only.

Think of it as insurance on a straight play — you give up some of the full straight payout for a fallback win if the order is wrong.

### Combo

The combo bet automatically plays all straight arrangements of your three digits on one ticket.

- Three distinct digits (like 4-7-2): 6 arrangements × $1 = **$6 total cost**, pays $500 if any arrangement hits
- Two matching digits (like 4-4-7): 3 arrangements × $1 = **$3 total cost**, pays $500 if any arrangement hits

Combo is effectively a box bet with straight payouts — you pay more per ticket but win at the straight rate.

| 3-way box | $160 | $80 |

## How to Fill Out a Play Slip

When you walk up to a NY Lottery terminal, you have two options:

**Quick pick** — tell the clerk you want a quick pick and how much you want to wager. The terminal randomly generates your digits. Fast and simple.

**Play slip** — pick up a play slip from the display rack near the terminal. Fill in:
1. Your three digits (one per box, positions 1, 2, 3)
2. Your bet type (Straight, Box, Straight/Box, or Combo)
3. Your wager amount ($0.50 or $1)
4. Which draw (Midday, Evening, or Both)
5. How many consecutive days to play (1–7 via Multi-Draw)

Hand the slip to the clerk, pay, and receive your printed ticket. **Always check your ticket immediately** before leaving the counter to confirm the digits and bet type are correct.

## How to Check Results

Official NY Pick 3 results are posted at:
- **nylottery.ny.gov** — official NY Lottery website
- **DailyNumberPicker.com** — pulls results directly from NY Open Data API
- Most local news stations post evening results

Midday results are typically posted around 3 PM. Evening results around 11 PM.

## Beginner Tips

**Start with box bets.** Until you have a feel for the game, box bets give you better odds and any-order coverage. A 6-way box at 1 in 167 is far more forgiving than straight at 1 in 1,000 while you're learning.

**Play both draws or just one.** If you want to keep costs down, pick your stronger analysis window — midday or evening — and play one draw per day rather than both.

**Use analysis before picking.** Random number picks or gut feelings produce systematically biased selections. Running a quick analysis on [DailyNumberPicker.com/pick3](/pick3) takes two minutes and gives you position-specific frequency data, skip tracking, and a composite digit ranking before every play.

**Track your plays.** Keep a simple log of what you played, the draw result, and what you won or lost. Over time this tells you which approaches are producing results.

**Set a budget and stick to it.** Pick 3 is entertainment. Set a fixed weekly budget and treat it like any other entertainment expense.

## The Difference Between Daily Numbers and Win 4

If you see "Win 4" at the lottery counter — that's the four-digit version of the same game. Same draw schedule (midday and evening), same bet types, but four digits instead of three. Straight odds are 1 in 10,000 with a $5,000 payout on $1.

Win 4 is harder to hit but pays more. Most beginners start with Daily Numbers (Pick 3) and add Win 4 once they understand the game structure.
